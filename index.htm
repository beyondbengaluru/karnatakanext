Why Emerging Clusters

The case for scaling beyond the capital

Four structural advantages make Karnataka's regional clusters the smartest expansion decision for technology companies.

Untapped Talent Pool

50+ engineering colleges and universities outside Bengaluru graduate a vast, under-tapped workforce hungry for tech careers close to home.

2.2 Lakh+ grads/yr

30–40% Lower Costs

Real estate, salaries, and operations cost dramatically less — turning the same budget into a far larger, more durable team.

Up to 40% savings

High Employee Retention

Hometown proximity and superior work-life balance cut attrition sharply versus metro hubs, protecting institutional knowledge.

~2x better retention

World-Class Social Infra

Airports, smart-city upgrades, healthcare, and international schools make relocation seamless for talent and leadership alike.

Getting Started

Approvals & incentives, simplified

Karnataka offers single-window clearances and one of India's most generous incentive regimes for IT, BT, ESDM, GCCs and Data Centres.

Know Your Approvals

  • Single-window clearance via Karnataka Udyog Mitra
  • Deemed approvals within statutory timelines
  • IT/BT units exempt from Standing Orders Act & inspections
  • Self-certification for labour & environmental norms
  • Dedicated relationship manager for anchor investors
